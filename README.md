### BCGT-000188: Paid verification when fraud is vanishingly rare
- **Subfield:** Proof markets, verification incentives and cryptographic mechanisms
- **Open:** Sustain independent verification in equilibrium when deterrence makes successful fraud rewards rare, accounting for free riding and outside attackers.
- **Status:** Research target since the verifier’s dilemma was articulated in 2015.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Maintains an economically credible verification assumption.
- **Key works:** Luu, Teutsch, Kulkarni and Saxena (2015), *Demystifying Incentives in the Consensus Computer*, IACR ePrint:2015/702.
- **Related:** Inspection games; volunteer’s dilemma with rare disasters.
